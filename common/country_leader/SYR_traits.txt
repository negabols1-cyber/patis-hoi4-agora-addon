# political power, cheaper improve relations upkeep; on hire, his on_add (SYR_friend_of_crowns_add_opinions in
# common/scripted_effects/SYR_scripted_effects.txt) raises opinion with
# monarchies and lowers it with democracies.
# SYR_warrior_sheikh is the country leader trait of SYR_muhammad_al_ashmar,
# leader of the Black Banner: more war support and army morale, less
# stability (same modifier-only shape as SYR_dislikes_the_ussr).
