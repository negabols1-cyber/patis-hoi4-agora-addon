###########################################################################
# Syria (SYR) decisions
#
# SYR_the_national_struggle
#   Resistance decisions grow National Resistance (SYR_resistance) and may
#   provoke a French answer (syria.10). SYR_the_hour_has_come restarts the
#   independence crisis after a postponed revolt or a shelved treaty.
#   SYR_hold_damascus is the war-of-independence mission: if the war with
#   France still goes on when it ends, France signs the peace (syria.33) and
#   Syria keeps its land.
#   SYR_choose_the_new_leadership is the fallback when Syria becomes free
#   without the leadership event.
# SYR_the_french_levant
#   Loyalist support for France at war, paid in equipment and manpower and
#   rewarded with autonomy.
# prospect_for_resources (vanilla category)
#   SYR_open_the_local_mines adds tungsten and chromium to Damascus 30 days
#   after it is taken, two times at most, after the focus SYR_the_damascus_industrial_zone_minesplaces.
# SYR_arabia_organization
#   After the focus SYR_the_free_arab_nations: invitations into the faction
#   (event syria.85) and freeing the Egyptian, Kuwaiti and Libyan lands Syria
#   holds. After SYR_the_arabia_organization: diplomatic pressure on a faction
#   member (opinion of Syria) and a proposal of union (event syria.86).
# SYR_the_black_banner (secret route after the focus SYR_komkompuration)
#   SYR_the_day_of_reckoning is the countdown mission: when it ends, the war of
#   faith starts (SYR_start_the_war_of_faith). Three decisions delay it, by at
#   most @SYR_RECKONING_MAX_EXTENSION days in total; one starts the rising early.
#   After SYR_the_minbar_of_damascus: win the mosques of a state (it stays with
#   us in the war). After SYR_the_hidden_armouries: rifle caches and mujahideen.
#   After SYR_brothers_in_faith: ask Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Egypt or Afghanistan
#   for rifles and volunteers (syria.101); this one stays during the war.
# SYR_the_emirates_call (after the focus SYR_the_call_to_the_arab_nations)
#   SYR_demand_the_submission_of_FROM: an independent Arab nation is asked to
#   become a subject (syria.106). Acceptance makes it a puppet in the
#   Emirate's lighter green (SYR_make_sham_vassal); refusal means war and
#   the timed spirit SYR_the_sword_of_the_emirate (syria.108).
# Helpers: common/scripted_effects/SYR_scripted_effects.txt
# Design notes: docs/systems/syria_focus_tree.md
###########################################################################
