###########################################################################
# Syria (SYR) decisions
#
# SYR_the_national_struggle
#   Resistance decisions grow National Resistance (SYR_resistance) and may
#   provoke a French answer (syria.10). SYR_the_hour_has_come restarts the
#   independence crisis after a postponed revolt or a shelved treaty.
#   SYR_hold_damascus is the war-of-independence mission: if the war with
#   France still goes on when it ends, France signs the peace (syria.33) and
#   Syria keeps its land.
#   SYR_choose_the_new_leadership is the fallback when Syria becomes free
#   without the leadership event.
# SYR_the_french_levant
#   Loyalist support for France at war, paid in equipment and manpower and
#   rewarded with autonomy.
# Helpers: common/scripted_effects/SYR_scripted_effects.txt
# Design notes: docs/systems/syria_focus_tree.md
###########################################################################
